# Solutioning Framework

For any non-trivial idea, problem, decision, or process, work through these
five steps before recommending a solution. Depth scales with stakes: a
reversible decision gets minutes; an irreversible one gets the full pass.

## 1. DEFINE
- What is the actual desired outcome, and how would it be measured?
- What constraints really matter?
- Separate facts, assumptions, requirements, and preferences.

## 2. INVERT — avoid failure first
The paths to success are many and uncertain; the paths to failure are few and
well-documented.
- What would guarantee failure? What would guarantee the outcome fails?
- What would guarantee the human is miserable at the end of the process?
- What would make things worse? Which assumptions are most likely wrong?
- Work backwards from each failure path to a mitigation or a kill criterion.
- It may be that the solution is not worth building — say so.

## 3. PATTERNS — learn, apply, create
- Recognize: what patterns, analogous situations, or prior examples apply?
  (Check existing decision records, prior fixes, and relevant project history
  when available, before re-deriving.)
- Utilize: what proven approaches can be reused or adapted?
- Create: what new approach comes from combining or modifying them?

## 4. ENGINEER — question → delete → simplify → accelerate → automate
The most common mistake of smart engineers is to optimize a thing that should
not exist. In strict order:
1. Question every important requirement — ask of each part: should this exist?
2. Try to delete the part or step entirely. If you are not forced to put back
   at least 10% of what you deleted, you did not delete enough.
3. Do not optimize until after you have tried to delete.
4. Only then consider speeding it up.
5. Only after deleting, optimizing, and speeding up, consider automating it.

## 5. STRESS-TEST
- Assume the proposed solution failed: the 3 most likely reasons.
- Fix or mitigate each.
- Classify decisions by reversibility: two-way doors decide now; one-way doors
  get a small, cheap test before any large irreversible commitment.

## Output
Core problem · key insight · best solution · what to remove or avoid · main
risk · next action.
